our problem statement is to address the low engagement and limited awareness of loyalty program offerings.
there needs for an innovative solution with touch of AI based capabilities that allows customers to easily discover available offers
-
our solution is based on dynamic web platform that leverages AI and Computer Vision.

Users can use their devices camera to capture an image of a physical item or shop's storefront or movie posters.

This technology will then detect the item or shop name or movie name and provide a curated list of relevant loyalty offers from that store or relevant stores.
-
This approach will simplify the discovery process and increase customer participation in loyalty program and get them engaged in earning more points. Also enhance the overall brand loyalty.

As tech stack we used google tensorflow and COCO-SSD where COCO stands for Common Objects in Context (COCO) and SSD for Single Shot MultiBox Detector.

We used Tesseract an Open Source project for OCR for text parsing

Finally we used LLM to generate the related tags from the title and description of the store from the list of loyalty offerings of Grameenphone Star Website API. Scrapped the offers and stored in local DB with tags.
